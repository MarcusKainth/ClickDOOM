**Status:** proposed

## Context

What forced the decision. The constraint, the measurement, or the conflict that
made the previous arrangement untenable. Someone who was not there should be
able to reconstruct why this came up.

## Decision

What was decided, stated so that a reviewer can tell whether a later change
contradicts it.

## Consequences

What this costs, what it rules out, and what has to be true for it to keep
holding. Name the alternative that was rejected and why.
